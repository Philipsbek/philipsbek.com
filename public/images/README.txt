Save your portrait photo in this folder as:

    philips-bek.jpg

That exact path (/images/philips-bek.jpg) is referenced in src/data/site.js
(site.photo) and is used on the About page and the home hero card.

Tips:
- A portrait/vertical crop works best (it's shown in a 4:5 frame).
- Optimize it (ideally under ~400 KB, around 800–1000px wide).
- To use a different filename or format (.png/.webp), update `photo`
  in src/data/site.js to match.
